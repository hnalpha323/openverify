# Phase 11 plan: resolution of the first review round

Three adversarial reviews read the first draft of `phase-11.plan.md` on 2026-10-29:
- privacy, security and law: P01–P35;
- honesty and product coverage: H01–H27;
- feasibility and fit with the code: F01–F30.

Together they raised 1 CRITICAL, 31 HIGH, 40 MEDIUM and 20 LOW findings, 92 in all:
- P: 8 HIGH, 16 MEDIUM, 11 LOW.
- H: 15 HIGH, 9 MEDIUM, 3 LOW. H27 bundles six small points.
- F: 1 CRITICAL, 8 HIGH, 15 MEDIUM, 6 LOW. F27 bundles six small points.

Dispositions: 88 accepted (P04 and F09 with another mechanism than the reviewer's, and H03, H07, H13, F15 and F18 with the option named below), 4 accepted in part (P08, P17, P24, H06, each with the part not taken and its evidence), none rejected outright. Two new owner items (Q11, Q12) record the licence exceptions the plan may not decide.

Every finding was checked against the repository at `6cceece3` (read-only; no build, test, formatter or git write was run) and against the Phase 5–10 plans that Phase 11 builds on. "→" names where the revised plan carries the fix, by contract item, Workflow or decision number. Items were added, never renumbered: a new item takes the next free number of its contract, after the honesty item where there is one.

Some findings were raised by more than one lens. Each is resolved once, under the first lens that raised it, and the others point there:
- P05 and F05 (the vision pod's weights and its egress);
- P06 and F20 (the step-up and SSO);
- P07 and F11's identity table;
- P14 and H08 (the built-in consent texts and counsel);
- P24 and F22 (the cross-tenant guard);
- P28 and F25 (the HSTS variable);
- H01 and F23 (client-side redemption's mode);
- H10 and F06 (the load scenarios against the rate limits);
- H11 and F01's timing rule (the measurement configuration).

Where a reviewer offered alternatives, the resolution names the option it took and why.

## Checks made for this resolution

These checks go beyond the reviewers' own evidence. Every one was read at `6cceece3`.

- **6D's state (H09).** `docs/calibration/` holds no `watchlist-phase6d.md`, and the newest Phase 6 entry of `docs/PROGRESS.md` is still Workflow 6C's; 6D's commits (`bd8adabd`, `3676bf17`, `6cceece3`) are landing. C271 stays conditional.
- **The sync's maintenance statements (F02).** `packages/db/src/repositories/watchlist-key-df.ts:30` runs `analyze watchlist_entries, watchlist_names`, and `:60-61` runs `gin_clean_pending_list` on `watchlist_names_keys_idx` and `watchlist_names_normalised_trgm_idx`. `watchlists.ts:537-539` analyses four list tables inside the sync's transaction. PostgreSQL 17 lets `ANALYZE` run under the `MAINTAIN` privilege; the GIN maintenance function stays with the index's owner or a superuser. The fix wraps the cleanup in a `SECURITY DEFINER` function, so it holds whichever way the privilege reads.

So the runtime does need a bounded way to delete audit rows after Phase 9, and the fix gives it a function instead of the table privilege.
