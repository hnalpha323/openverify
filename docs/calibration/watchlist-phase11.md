# Watchlist matching: the Workflow 11D calibration (C270, C271, C269.4)

This report measures the matcher's write cost (C270), the C78.7 budgets and the re-screen at
Workflow 11D's base and head (C271.3; review H09), the screening during a 2,000,000-name sync
(C269.4), and the share of each switchable change Stage 2 made. It is written by
`calibration-matcher-11d`, alone on the machine, after `calibration-load-11d` has stopped the load
stack. **Status: published** (results below). The Method below was committed by `contracts-11d` (the commit whose
subject contains "Workflow 11D contracts-11d checkpoint method") before any take and before the seed
was drawn; a change made after it is listed under "Amendments" with its reason.

Every number here comes from synthetic data. Synthetic variation is not real-world variation: the
names, their frequencies and the customers are generated by rules. Write-cost figures are for the
synthetic development tables; real publications change in other shapes (C270.5).

## Method

### What is compared (C271.3)

- **Base:** 11D's base, `d44e537dba0f84a4eafc45b313264de74c2b31a4` (Workflow 11C's close as pushed).
- **Head:** the head Stage 2 leaves (the commit after `matcher-write-11d`'s and `matcher-c787-11d`'s
  last checkpoints), pinned by `calibration-matcher-11d` at its start and named here.
- **One benchmark at both trees,** as 6D: the base carries the head's
  `packages/watchlist/scripts/benchmark-*` and `packages/watchlist/src/testing/heavy-headed.ts`
  overlaid uncommitted, checked byte for byte with `sha256sum -c`; the head's switch registry
  (`packages/db/src/repositories/watchlist-switches.ts`) is imported only by the steps that need it,
  so the base runs every other step.
- **Two worktrees** under `/tmp` (`/tmp/cal11d-base`, `/tmp/cal11d-head`), never the main tree, each
  prepared with `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile --offline`, the `data/` files copied except
  `variant-groups.json`, and its own variant groups built with its own script; their SHA-256 recorded.
- **Namespaces:** `c11d-base`, `c11d-head`, and `-r2`, `-r3` for the second and third re-screen
  databases; each tree's own `--namespace`, never reused.

### The configuration (6D's; C271.3)

The Testcontainers Postgres the benchmark starts (`packages/watchlist/scripts/benchmark-postgres.mjs`,
its image by digest, `POSTGRES_TEST_COMMAND`'s C73.8 flags), **the load stack stopped**, and nothing
else running: no test suite, build, validation or other agent. Every take is run from
`<tree>/packages/watchlist` under `flock -E 75 -w 240 /tmp/openverify-e2e.lock`, one step per lock
hold.

### The seed (independence)

One fresh evaluation seed, drawn by `calibration-matcher-11d` with `openssl rand -hex 16` **after this
method is committed**, and used at both trees, so every difference is paired. Nothing generated is
committed: tables, cases, builds and per-query results stay under `/tmp/openverify-eval-11d/` and
`packages/watchlist/results/` (outside git); only aggregates are published here. The held-out folder
is read only by the benchmark's own evaluation steps.

**Seed dependence (6D Stage 1 and 2 amendments item 12).** No owner decision says whether C78.7 is
judged over several seeds, so it is judged on this one seed, as 6D's was, and the report prints beside
each verdict the gate's figure on the 6C seed's table (`GATE_SEED`) and states that the figure
depends on the draw (6D measured 180.9 ms on its seed and 65.0 ms on the 6C seed's table with the
same head). The question stays open for the owner.

### The timing rule (6C's and 6D's, unchanged)

- A timing counts only when the 1-minute load average is at most **4 at its start and at its end**;
  otherwise it is discarded and retaken when the machine is quieter. Before each timing the runner
  waits inside the lock for the load to fall below 2.5 (2.0 before a re-screen, share or gate take).
- **Three takes and the median**, with the loads; the pooled p95 beside it. Counts (identity, runs,
  cap, truncation, listed found, alerts) do not depend on the machine and are read from the first
  take. A take that never meets the load rule after repeated waits is reported with its loads and
  marked; no verdict rests on it alone.
- **The long-take exception** (review F01): a step that cannot be split into calls of under 10
  minutes (the 2,000,000-name re-key confirmation, a sync during which screening is measured) runs in
  the background under the e2e lock with a heartbeat file every 10 s and an end marker, alone; its
  start, end and largest heartbeat gap are recorded beside its figure.

### What is measured

1. **The identity check** (6D decision 8), keyed by query and preset, through
   `benchmark:quality --step identity` and `benchmark:performance --step identity`: the kept entry
   ids, `fixed`, the coverage `limits`, the `errors` and the SHA-256 of the full created matches (6D's
   digest), for the 6B set and the scale population at both scales, base against head.
2. **The executed runs** after each `rescreen-<n>` at both trees, and `rescreen-check --size 1000`
   (no miss), as 6D.
3. **C78.7**, the screening p95 of the `watchlist` job at `tolerant` with the shipped groups, at
   100,000 and 2,000,000 names, three takes each of `<scale>-measure --rounds 1`, the base's and the
   head's takes alternating; the phase split (state / retrieval / loads / the rest).
4. **The re-screen** at 100, 300 and 1,000 changed entries and 200,000 subjects, three takes per size
   and tree, each take on its own fresh database; the run cost and the total cost as 6D defined them.
5. **The gate**, three times at the head with `OPENVERIFY_C787_TIMING=assert` (from `apps/api`:
   `pnpm exec vitest run --config vitest.integration.config.ts src/watchlists/watchlists.integration.test.ts`),
   its p95 beside the head's 100,000-name measurement.
6. **The shares** of each switch Stage 2 added (Stage 1a amendment 7): each switch **off and on
   alternately within one lock hold**, at the scale it targets and at the other, three takes (`--takes
   3`), the share the median of (on − off) with its range, every round's digests compared with the
   priming round's. The C271 levers (`retrieval-reach`, `stored-name-analysis`, `rescreen-bound-memo`,
   `rescreen-prepared-index`) are measured on the screening or re-screen step they change.
7. **C270.1, the incremental key frequencies** (`incremental-key-df`): the identity test (after a
   seeded sequence of syncs with adds, removals, renames and variant-group changes, the frequencies
   the switch maintains equal a full rebuild's exactly, at both scales on development tables, and every
   created match is identical by the digest), then its timing: the sync of a 1,000-entry change on the
   2,000,000-name table with the switch off and on, three takes each, alternating.
8. **C270.2, the re-key's batch size** (`--tune rekey-batch=<n>`): 500, 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000
   entries a batch at 100,000 names, three takes each, with the process's peak memory (its largest
   RSS and V8 heap). **The chosen size is the fastest median whose peak heap stays within 2 GiB;** it
   is then confirmed **once** at 2,000,000 names against the current size (500), one take each (the
   long-take exception). The re-key's output must be identical (the digest of every derived column)
   and it stays resumable.
9. **C270.3 and C269.4, the GIN pending lists:** on the 2,000,000-name table, the screening p95
   while a 2,000,000-name sync runs (the last part of the 2,000,000-name import: one sync writing
   about 250,000 names onto 1,750,000), against the idle screening p95, and the sync's own time, for the status quo and each option: `gin-fastupdate-off`,
   `--tune gin-pending-list-limit=<kB>` (256, 1,024) and `--tune gin-clean-every=<n>` (10, 50 batches),
   three takes each (the long-take exception). **The option taken** is the one that keeps the
   screening p95 during the sync **within 2 times idle at the lowest sync cost**; if none does, none
   is taken, the status quo stays and the figures are reported (H27). Its shipping is Stage 1a
   amendment 12.

### Budgets (owner decision 1 after 6C, unchanged)

- C78.7 at 100,000 names: a median round p95 of at most **100 ms**.
- C78.7 at 2,000,000 names: at most **250 ms**.
- The re-screen at 1,000 changed entries and 200,000 subjects: a median of at most **60 s**.
- Small syncs: at 100 and 300 changed entries, the head's total cost no higher than the base's.

Each budget is **met** or **missed** with its gap **at the head**, and the head's verdict is the
release's (C271.4). A budget still missed is an open line of the release checklist with both figures
and its profile, named in the release notes. A head miss where 6D met reopens the C270 change that
caused it under its switch (off until fixed).

### Decision rules (fixed before the seed)

**The margin is 5%** of the reference p95 (the switch off for a share, the base for a change that
cannot be switched), as 6D: three takes cannot tell a smaller difference from this machine's noise.

1. **Identity:** `identical` when every (query, preset) pair at both scales matches in the kept ids,
   `fixed`, `limits`, `errors` and the digest; otherwise `not-identical`, with the differing pairs
   counted by field. Any difference is a defect for `fix-11d`, never a trade.
2. **A switchable change is `kept`** when its digests are identical at both scales and its median
   (on − off) is at most +5% of its off p95 at each scale (it is not worse by more than 5% at either
   scale); otherwise **`revert`** (its switch stays off), naming its commits.
3. **A change that cannot be switched** is `kept` when the identity check is identical and the head's
   median round p95 is at most 5% above the base's at both scales; otherwise `revert`.
4. **The re-screen changes** are `kept` when the executed runs are kept, `rescreen-check` finds no
   miss at 1,000 entries, the head inserts the same runs as the base at each size, and the head's
   median at 1,000 entries is at most 5% above the base's.
5. **C270.1** is `kept` when its identity test holds at both scales and the sync with the switch on is
   not slower by more than 5% than with it off; **C270.2**'s size is chosen by item 8's rule;
   **C270.3**'s option by item 9's rule.
6. **The gate:** three takes at the head with the timing asserted; it names the case
   `shipped-variant-groups` and passes its machine-independent checks.
7. **Each budget:** met or missed with its gap (above).
