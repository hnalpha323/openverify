Agent: `workflow-connect` (family U), beside `api-connect`, `api-redeem`, `api-consent`, `clients-connect` and `console-connect` (rule 23: it gates only what it touches: `@openverify/api`). It owns `apps/api/src/workflows/actions/connect/` and its tests (Stage 1a amendment 15). C243: the actions `create_share_token`, `redeem_share_token` and `expire_share_token` on Phase 8's engine, and the triggers `connect/share-token.inbound-created`, `consent-record.withdrawn` and `connect/share-token.consent-withdrawn`.
