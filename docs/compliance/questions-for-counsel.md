# Questions for counsel

This is not legal advice.

Not reviewed by counsel.

Counsel's review of this guide and of the built-in `share.consent.*` texts is the owner's item Q7, pending. Until it is recorded, every page of the guide says "Not reviewed by counsel.", and the built-in share-consent texts carry the review status `counsel-unreviewed` (C238.10). This page lists the questions that review should answer: first the three Q7 names (C278.4), then every inference the guide makes in our own reading.

## Q7's questions

1. **Withdrawal through the organisation.** Once the flow has ended, withdrawal of a Connect consent goes through the organisation's own contact (C237.4). Does operator-mediated withdrawal meet the rule that withdrawing must be as easy as consenting (Article 7(3); [sharing](sharing.md))?
2. **Face templates in the access export.** The export lists face templates as present with their count and does not export them (C253.3). Does that exclusion hold under the right of access ([access requests](access-requests.md))?
3. **The `operator-review` part.** Which exemptions (for example tipping off under section 333A of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002, or their equivalents elsewhere) govern the cases, list memberships, signals and integrity verdicts labelled "review before disclosure" ([access requests](access-requests.md))?

## Inferences in the guide

4. **Roles.** The operator is the controller and, for its clients, their processor; the OpenVerify project is neither ([roles](roles.md)).
5. **A hosting operator and Connect.** A hosting operator that is processor for two organisations moves data between them only on both controllers' instructions ([roles](roles.md)).
6. **The strictly-necessary exemption** for session integrity, probe by probe ([device and network data](device-and-network.md); [session integrity](session-integrity.md)).
7. **One-to-many face matching under the EU AI Act.** Are `selfie_repeat_detection` and face lists "remote biometric identification" in Annex III 1(a)'s sense ([biometric data](biometric-data.md))?
8. **Washington's photograph exclusion.** Is a face template computed from a selfie photograph "data generated from" a photograph under RCW 19.375.010 ([US state laws](us-state-laws.md))?
9. **Colorado's thresholds.** Do HB24-1130's biometric duties apply whatever the Colorado Privacy Act's usual applicability thresholds ([US state laws](us-state-laws.md))?
10. **Restricted transfers.** When is a Connect share between two organisations on one install a restricted transfer, and which Chapter V route fits ([sharing](sharing.md))?
11. **Solely automated decisions.** Which OpenVerify decisions, with your review practice, are significant decisions based solely on automated processing under the UK's Articles 22A to 22D or the EU's Article 22 ([README](README.md#automated-decisions))?
12. **The built-in notice and consent texts.** Do the default texts of ADR 0006 and the `share.consent.*` keys carry what each law asks for (owner decision 8 after Workflow 7A; C238.10)?
