## The internal route on the public listener (C250.3)

- `GET /api/v1/internal/embed-policies/{inquiryId}` on the API's public listener answered **401** without the flow server's token. The e2e stack leaves OPENVERIFY_INTERNAL_LISTEN unset, so the internal routes stay on the public listener behind the flow server's token; C250.3 moves them off it once it is set.

## What ZAP does not cover (C283.3)

- Cross-tenant authorisation: its evidence is C240.2's authorisation matrix and the generated cross-tenant probe (C240.7), not this scan.
- Business logic: Connect's consent and redemption, sessions, quotas and the review rules are held by their own tests.
- The flow's later steps (capture, documents, selfies, NFC): the API scan reaches their routes with generated values, not a real verification.
- The console behind real MFA: the scanned console session is a seeded admin's, supplied through a replacer rule, so no second factor is exercised.
- ZAP is a regression net for headers, error bodies and injection.

## Re-running one scan

Each subcommand of `security/zap/run.sh` is one call under the e2e lock, on the reusable e2e stack in its images mode: `prepare`, `api [group]`, `flow [hosted|noguidance|routes]`, `console`, `report`, `revoke` (`bash security/zap/run.sh` runs them all in order).
