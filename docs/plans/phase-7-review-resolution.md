# Phase 7 plan: review resolution

Three adversarial reviews read the first draft of `docs/plans/phase-7.plan.md` on 2026-10-27, each read-only and independent:
- privacy, security and law (P01–P29);
- honesty and product coverage (H01–H32);
- feasibility and fit with the code at `e03387fa` (F01–F41).

Together they found 5 CRITICAL, 27 HIGH, 43 MEDIUM and 27 LOW: 102 in all. This file disposes of each one. No finding was rejected. Parts of nine are met by an explicit deferral in C110, each with its reason (H17, H19, H20, H22, H27, H28, H30, and the public behaviours they name). Six take a different route from the reviewer's proposal, and their rows say why: P06, P13, P26, H32, F03 and F35.

The review files themselves are kept with the research downloads, outside git. Their text is summarised here.
